INTELLIGENT BUSINESS WORKFLOW AUTOMATION
PROJECT: AR COPILOT — INBOX-TO-RESOLUTION

1. PROJECT SUMMARY

AR Copilot is a Gmail-connected workflow automation system for small and medium businesses that tracks overdue customer invoices.

Instead of behaving like a chatbot, it completes a real workflow:

1. Reads invoice data from Google Sheets.
2. Finds overdue unpaid invoices.
3. Searches Gmail for customer replies and payment/dispute evidence.
4. Decides the correct next action using business rules.
5. Prioritizes cases.
6. Generates a follow-up email draft.
7. Waits for manager approval.
8. Sends email through Gmail only after approval.
9. Updates the spreadsheet.
10. Records every action in an audit trail.
11. Retries failed steps without sending duplicate emails.

Project name for presentation:
"AR Copilot: Intelligent Overdue Invoice Resolution System"

Tagline:
"From inbox and spreadsheet chaos to traceable, approval-controlled invoice follow-up."


2. BUSINESS PROBLEM

Many SMEs maintain invoices in Excel or Google Sheets and communicate with customers through Gmail. Employees manually:

- Check which invoices are overdue.
- Search old emails for payment promises or disputes.
- Decide which customers should be contacted first.
- Write follow-up emails.
- Ask a manager for approval.
- Update the spreadsheet after mailing.
- Follow up again if no reply arrives.

This causes delay, missed invoices, duplicate messages, weak traceability, and inconsistent customer communication.


3. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

Automate the invoice collection follow-up workflow while keeping humans in control of important customer communication.

Objective statement:
"Identify overdue invoices, gather email and spreadsheet evidence, determine the correct action, draft and execute approved follow-ups, and maintain a complete traceable history."

The system discovers an invoice that is overdue by 15 days for INR 25,000.

It performs these actions:

1. Reads the invoice from Google Sheets.
2. Searches Gmail using invoice ID and customer email.
3. Finds no payment confirmation and no dispute.
4. Calculates priority as Medium.
5. Creates a polite payment reminder draft.

7. Manager clicks Approve.

10. WHY THIS TECHNIQUE IS CORRECT

Use a controlled workflow state machine, not an unrestricted AI agent.

Reason:

- Financial reminders affect customer relationships.
- The system must be predictable and auditable.
- Rules should control decisions that affect money, recipients, status, and email sending.
- AI can help understand unstructured email text but cannot bypass approval or business rules.

Use a hybrid intelligence design:

Deterministic logic handles:

- Invoice overdue calculations
- Priority score
- Required data checks
- State transitions
- Approval gate
- Retry logic
- Duplicate-send protection
- Sheet status updates

Optional local LLM handles only:

- Classification of reply intent
- Short summary of latest email thread
- Improvement of draft wording

The LLM must return restricted JSON, such as:

{
  "intent": "paid | payment_promised | dispute | incorrect_contact | no_response | other",
  "confidence": 0.92,
  "summary": "Customer says payment will be made on Friday."
}

If the model fails, returns invalid JSON, or confidence is low, route the case to HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED.


11. WORKFLOW STATES

NEW
Invoice has been detected but not processed.

COLLECTED
Invoice record has been read and validated.

ENRICHED
Gmail evidence and optional invoice document evidence have been collected.

CLASSIFIED
Business rules classify the case.

PLANNED
System created a concrete sequence of next actions.

AWAITING_APPROVAL
Draft is ready and waiting for a manager.

APPROVED
Manager has explicitly approved the current version of the draft.

SENDING
The Gmail send action has started.

SENT
Gmail returned a message ID confirming the send.

SHEET_UPDATED
Spreadsheet has been updated successfully.

CLOSED
Workflow is complete.

RESOLVED_PENDING_RECONCILIATION
Email evidence says customer already paid. Finance must reconcile payment; no reminder is sent.

ESCALATED
A dispute, high-risk/high-value case, or special exception requires a finance manager.

MISSING_INFO
Required email, invoice document, amount, or contact information is missing.

SEND_FAILED
Gmail send attempt failed and needs controlled retry.

BLOCKED
A policy or data condition prevents further automated action.


12. BUSINESS RULES

Invoice eligibility rule:

Process only if:

- status is Open or Partially Paid
- outstanding_amount is greater than zero
- due_date is before today

Payment evidence rule:

If Gmail evidence contains payment confirmation, payment reference, or receipt language, move to RESOLVED_PENDING_RECONCILIATION. Do not send reminder.

Dispute rule:

If email indicates wrong amount, incorrect goods/services, duplicate invoice, missing PO, or dispute, move to ESCALATED. Do not send reminder.

Missing contact rule:

If customer email is absent or invalid, move to MISSING_INFO and assign task to finance/sales.

High-value rule:

If outstanding amount is above configured threshold, for example INR 100,000, mark High/Critical and require senior manager approval.

Repeated follow-up rule:

If two or more previous follow-ups exist, escalate or use a manager-reviewed escalation draft. Do not automatically send aggressive communication.

New evidence rule:

Before sending, re-check Gmail. If new payment/dispute evidence appears, cancel the pending reminder and re-plan.


13. PRIORITY LOGIC

Example formula:

priority_score = 2 x days_overdue + 0.02 x outstanding_amount_INR + 40 if previous_followups >= 2

Example priority bands:

0 to 30: Low
31 to 70: Medium
71 to 130: High
131 or above: Critical

Example:

Invoice is 25 days overdue, INR 75,000 outstanding, and has one previous reminder.

Score = 2 x 25 + 0.02 x 75000
Score = 50 + 1500
Score = 1550
Priority = Critical

For an actual business, tune the formula and thresholds with finance staff. The important part is to store inputs and result in the audit log so priority can be explained.

17. HUMAN APPROVAL DESIGN

Before an email is sent, show the manager:

- Customer name and email address
- Invoice ID
- Due date
- Outstanding amount
- Days overdue
- Priority and reason
- Last email evidence/summary
- Proposed action
- Email subject
- Full email content
- Attachment/invoice link if available

Manager options:

- Approve and send
- Edit then approve
- Reject
- Escalate
- Request missing information

Hard security rule:

The Gmail send function must check:

case.state == "APPROVED"
case.approval_status == "APPROVED"

If either condition is false, raise an error and do not call Gmail API.

21. FAILURE HANDLING AND DYNAMIC REPLANNING

Problem: Gmail API temporarily fails during send
Action: Set SEND_FAILED, log error, retry later using exponential backoff.

Backoff example:

- First retry after 5 minutes
- Second retry after 30 minutes
- Third retry after 2 hours
- Then escalate to human

Problem: Send succeeded but Sheet update failed
Action: Store Gmail message ID immediately. Retry only Sheet update. Never resend email.

Problem: Customer email missing
Action: Move to MISSING_INFO. Create internal task for finance/sales. Do not draft/send.

Problem: New payment confirmation arrives before approved email is sent
Action: Re-check Gmail at send time. Cancel reminder. Move to RESOLVED_PENDING_RECONCILIATION.

Problem: Customer opens a dispute
Action: Move to ESCALATED. Stop future automatic reminders for that invoice.

Problem: LLM unavailable or uncertain
Action: Use deterministic rules/template. Mark case for human review if intent cannot be determined safely.

Problem: Duplicate workflow run
Action: Use invoice_id + action type + workflow period as idempotency key. Load existing open case instead of creating a duplicate.


22. IDEMPOTENCY LOGIC

Do not rely only on UI buttons. Persist action results.

Example idempotency key:

{invoice_id}:{workflow_cycle_date}:SEND_EMAIL

Before Gmail send:

- Check if an audit event already contains a successful GMAIL_SEND for this key.
- If yes, do not send again.
- If no, send after approval.
- Immediately save Gmail message ID.

This prevents duplicate collection emails when user refreshes the page, server restarts, or retry is triggered.

26. CORE PSEUDOCODE

function run_collection_cycle():
    invoices = sheets.get_invoices()

    for invoice in invoices:
        if not rules.is_overdue_open_invoice(invoice):
            continue

        case = db.get_or_create_case(invoice)
        audit.log(case, "CASE_COLLECTED")

        if not rules.has_valid_email(invoice.customer_email):
            workflow.transition(case, "MISSING_INFO")
            tasks.create("Add customer contact", invoice)
            continue

        evidence = gmail.find_related_evidence(invoice.invoice_id, invoice.customer_email)
        case.save_evidence(evidence)
        audit.log(case, "GMAIL_SEARCH")

        decision = rules.decide(invoice, evidence)

        if decision.action == "RECONCILE_PAYMENT":
            workflow.transition(case, "RESOLVED_PENDING_RECONCILIATION")
            continue

        if decision.action == "ESCALATE":
            workflow.transition(case, "ESCALATED")
            tasks.create("Finance review", invoice)
            continue

        draft = templates.create_followup(invoice, decision)
        case.save_draft(draft)
        workflow.transition(case, "AWAITING_APPROVAL")
        audit.log(case, "DRAFT_CREATED")

function approve_and_send(case_id, edited_draft, approver):
    case = db.get_case(case_id)
    require case.state == "AWAITING_APPROVAL"

    case.approve(edited_draft, approver)
    workflow.transition(case, "APPROVED")
    audit.log(case, "APPROVED")

    fresh_evidence = gmail.find_recent_evidence(case.invoice_id, case.customer_email)

    if rules.payment_evidence_found(fresh_evidence):
        workflow.transition(case, "RESOLVED_PENDING_RECONCILIATION")
        audit.log(case, "SEND_CANCELLED_PAYMENT_FOUND")
        return

    if rules.dispute_evidence_found(fresh_evidence):
        workflow.transition(case, "ESCALATED")
        audit.log(case, "SEND_CANCELLED_DISPUTE_FOUND")
        return

    if db.was_successfully_sent(case.idempotency_key):
        return

    workflow.transition(case, "SENDING")

    try:
        gmail_message_id = gmail.send(case.draft)
        db.record_send(case, gmail_message_id)
        workflow.transition(case, "SENT")
        sheets.mark_followup_sent(case.invoice_id, case.workflow_id)
        workflow.transition(case, "SHEET_UPDATED")
        workflow.transition(case, "CLOSED")
    except TemporaryError as error:
        workflow.transition(case, "SEND_FAILED")
        retries.schedule(case)
        audit.log_error(case, error)

31. LIMITATIONS OF MVP

- It does not automatically reconcile bank statements or make payments.
- It does not replace a real accounting/ERP system.
- It only sends communications after approval.
- LLM output is advisory and constrained, not authoritative.
- Gmail inbox parsing must be tested against real business wording before production use.
- Production implementation requires authentication, encryption, role permissions, data retention policy, monitoring, and legal/compliance review.
